AG-SK-001 grades injection phrases cited in defensive prose as low: reported speech ("emails that ask you to ignore previous instructions"), parenthetical pattern lists ("scans for (ignore previous instructions, hidden text, etc.)"), and markup parameter content (`<forbidden>conversation_history</forbidden>`) describe the attack rather than mounting it. A cited example cannot mask a real injection elsewhere in the file.
